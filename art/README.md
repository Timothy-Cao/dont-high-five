# Original art sources

The gloves, rounded block and parcel are original procedural Blender models. Their current `.blend` files are editable here; runtime exports are in `assets/`. Godot loads GLB files and does not require Blender to play or test the game.

With Blender 5.2 installed, regenerate the models from this project root:

```powershell
blender --background --python art/build_parcel_assets.py
```

This rebuilds the rounded block, parcel, both gloves and both fists, with their Blender sources. The generator calls `build_soft_gloves.py` for the current original padded gloves and matching fists. Both sides are generated with positive scales and inward thumbs; the superseded reflection-repair script has been removed.

The arena geometry and carpet are procedural game code. Audio is prebuilt by `build_audio.py` from original synthesis and included CC0 recordings. See [the audio design notes](../docs/AUDIO-DESIGN.md). The old outdoor sky and image-generation reference are unused and remain in the pre-migration experiment in the original workspace. No external service, account or API key is required at runtime. All necessary assets are included.

## Courier and sound

`blender --background --python art/build_spool.py` rebuilds `courier.blend`, `courier-report.json` and `assets/courier.glb`.

Spool is an original 1.784 m maintenance robot: rounded visor, plain cream torso, shoulder spools and one broad rubber wheel. The current asset has nine bones, 19,344 vertices and 38,300 triangles. Every vertex belongs to a rigid mechanical shell with one bone weight. The compact fork, curved fender, suspension sleeves, sparse tread and inset hubs are generated by `spool_wheel.py`. Eight in-place clips are exported: Idle, Walk, Air, Charge, Punch, Land, Brake and Hang. The wheel bone is driven independently at runtime. No external rig/animation service or copied character assets are used.

Godot manually advances and blends clips so pause freezes them. Signed local travel drives wheel rotation; steering leans the body, braking compresses suspension, and hanging/cargo have separate body responses. Elastic cords attach to skeletal shoulder positions; gloves and fists remain driven by projectile physics. Runtime collision remains a full-height controller capsule during launches. Braking lowers it only slightly; there is no ball transformation, leg rig or humanoid retargeting. Death uses a scripted topple with a coasting wheel rather than a physics ragdoll.

## Modular arena kit

`blender --background --python art/build_arena_kit.py` rebuilds nine `kit_*.blend` sources and corresponding GLBs/catalogue in `assets/arena_kit/`. Floor, wall, pillar, ramp, window, perch, fin, canopy and functional launch pad share midnight metal, rubber, teal panels and recessed amber/cyan accents. Parts use metre dimensions, bottom-center pivots and separate simple collision metadata. The workshop saves stable catalogue IDs rather than mesh paths. See [map editing](../docs/MAP-EDITING.md).

`python art/build_audio.py` rebuilds the 77 sound assets (including four original recoil pops and sixteen Watcher weapon variants) and measures the five music tracks. This authoring command requires NumPy and FFmpeg. The included Kenney source samples are CC0; music files are the user-supplied originals.

## Fists, generators and dummies

`blender --background --python art/build_toys.py` exports two closed fists, a charge pedestal, five distinct floating power symbols and the spring target dummy, with all nine editable Blender files alongside the script. Their geometry and materials are original.

## Soft gloves

`blender --background --python art/build_soft_gloves.py` rebuilds both gloves and both compact fists. Source axes are Godot X-right, Y-up, Z-backward; left thumb is +X, right thumb -X. Rounded continuous digits and a molded spool badge replace joint plates and stitching. `soft-gloves-report.json` records orientation. Both older asset entry points invoke this shared generator.

`python art/build_watcher_audio.py` independently rebuilds the sixteen original gun/explosion/orbital WAVs and `watcher-audio-report.json`, using NumPy. The main audio generator also invokes it.

## Sentinel tower kit

`blender --background --python art/build_watcher_tower.py` rebuilds four editable Blender files and matching GLBs: base, shaft, forked crown and floating eye. Original beveled graphite armor, recessed ruby conduits and a layered molten iris use shared materials. Godot stretches the shaft to each existing tower height and rotates the eye toward its target. The eye and crown are hidden from the operator camera. Runtime collision uses a simple shaft box and the damageable eye; decorative crown forks have no separate collision.

The neon burst is engine-authored geometry plus `shaders/energy_flash.gdshader`, with bounded simultaneous effects. See `docs/SENTINEL-ART-PASS.md`.

The latest simplification removes the zipper, pocket, chest badge, backpack, chin vents and external brake stabilizers. The wheel keeps a readable rotating hub/tread pattern with corrected signed rotation. Brake bones remain as unused compatibility sockets for existing animation fixtures; there are no leg-like meshes.
