# Tomorrow's discussion — quests and an overpowered hunter

User notes recorded 21 September 2026. **Ideas to discuss, not instructions to implement.** No gameplay changes are authorized by these notes.

## The user's proposed direction

Good players complete quests that use the playground and movement:

- Travel through particular hoops.
- Collect items.
- Take down target dummies.
- Carry a ball to a destination.

**Ball carrying:** one hand holds the ball; the other remains available for pulling/grappling. Punching is unavailable while carrying because it requires both hands. This creates a choice between transporting an objective, moving with one hand, and freeing both hands to fight. Dropping, throwing and passing behavior remain undecided.

The antagonist has access to a varied, powerful toolset. Examples the user wants to discuss:

- Sniper rifles.
- Flight.
- Delayed explosions affecting areas of the map.
- Laser vision.
- Other tools with different ways to threaten players.

Good players respond by maintaining fast movement, staying hidden, or fighting back. Their punch can **knock out the antagonist without killing them**. Knockout duration, recovery and the antagonist's remaining options are not yet specified.

The intended contrast is a mobile, evasive team completing activities against a much more heavily equipped opponent, with nonlethal opportunities to turn the tables. These examples are a pool of possibilities, not a commitment to give every tool simultaneously or build a large weapon catalogue now.

## Questions to bring to the discussion

These are preparation questions, not decisions or requests for an answer tonight.

1. **Hidden traitor, revealed hunter, or a transition between them?** The new arsenal makes an overt chase game a serious possibility. Do not force these notes into the earlier Blackout Relay proposal.
2. **Which quests produce the best movement?** Compare a hoop route, a dummy encounter and a ball delivery. Do they encourage interesting crossings and temporary exposure, or merely send people to markers?
3. **How much freedom does cargo remove?** One-hand grappling is an explicit part of the idea. Discuss whether dropping the ball to punch is fast and readable, and whether another player can help move it.
4. **What warning and response belongs to each threat?** Explore sightlines and cover for a rifle, escape routes for a delayed blast, and how flight changes pursuit. The user explicitly proposed a delay for area explosions; warning duration and presentation are still open.
5. **What does knocking out the antagonist buy the team?** A window to deliver, rescue or relocate could be enough. Discuss how the antagonist recovers and how repeated punches avoid becoming a permanent lockout.
6. **Where does the high-five fit now?** It might assist an escape, pass something useful, or remain a social gesture. Its role should earn a place in this direction rather than require unrelated battery rules.
7. **What ends a round?** Quest quota, individual completion, team escape, opponent success and consequences for caught good players remain unresolved.
