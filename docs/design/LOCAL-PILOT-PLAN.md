# Local High Fiver / Watcher pilot

2026-09-21. User approved continuing the full brief in realistic stages: local role switching, optional tower auto-fire OFF by default, separate Training with Arena directly accessible. No networking or public deployment is part of this pass.

North star: complete a physical job while choosing a fast exposed route or a slower sheltered one. Fantasy: nimble glove robots evade an overwhelming but spatially constrained Watcher. Primary verb: place a hand and commit to a route. Pleasure: a clean traversal chain, a useful assist, a last-second escape. Tension: task locations expose you to attention. Payoff: a delivered object or completed task visibly changes the shared quota.

Fun hypothesis: carrying a ball leaves enough movement options that the player enjoys choosing a one-handed route; readable tower telegraphs make escaping feel earned rather than random. Automated tests cannot establish this.

Implementation sequence:
1. Hand occupancy, physical cargo, nonlethal recipient contract, health/respawn, punch/zip selection.
2. Separate enclosed Training route with bindings, recovery and skip access.
3. Local task sampler: rings, cargo, dummy KO, maintenance, reciprocal high-five test partner.
4. Seven switchable Watcher eyes, guns, delayed blast, bouncing grenade, blackout, optional auto-fire/full-auto, eye disruption, infiltration test.
5. One maze region, structural corridors, upper refuge routes, exposed stations, configurable hazard/powerup examples, scene-authored map markers.
6. Integration tests, authored render review, source cleanup, package and point-by-point disposition.

Preserve current movement contracts. No purchases, installs, desktop/browser automation or public push. Existing original robot, glove and sound assets are reused; code-built eyes, warning rings and task props remain original.

References inspected: official Panoptic, BOSSGARD and DAVIGO Steam descriptions (not gameplay sessions). Panoptic's slow-charging eye against a hidden Challenger suggests a readable wind-up; its one-hit stealth model does not transfer to this movement prototype. BOSSGARD suggests strong asymmetric roles but does not establish balance. DAVIGO's separate big/small perspectives suggest testing both views locally; its VR physics is not copied. Existing movement research remains in MOVEMENT-MODES.md. Direct sources will be retained in the final review.

## Outcome and next playtest

The six stages above now have a local implementation. [Review](../LOCAL-PILOT.md) records verification and limits; [disposition](IMPLEMENTATION-BACKLOG.md) covers the original notes individually. A separate verified movement snapshot was published as v0.2.0 before continuing this broader work.

Cheapest useful human test: spend five minutes in Training, then carry the ball using the free arm; next turn on unattended fire and repeat. Ask when the player first felt that their route choice mattered, whether the hand occupancy was understood, and whether a missed high-five or zip produced an unwanted action. Switch to Watcher and ask which targets can actually be recognized. Do not interpret bot hits or an occlusion percentage as proof of fun or fair multiplayer.

Skill evaluation: three direction questions changed scope, AI defaults and launch navigation. Research linked movement, physical cooperation and asymmetric pursuit; readable warnings, optional pressure and preserving genuine refuges follow from those comparisons. Main playable payoff is a seven-task quota, with distinct practice rooms and reset routes. Assets are original and local; external image retrieval was not sufficient for competitor visual claims. Automated and rendered evidence is separated from human enjoyment. An unrelated puzzle-pilot dry run would retain questions → evidence → small arc → real render review; a research-only brief would stop before implementation. No skill change was justified by those checks, and no delegation was authorized or used.
